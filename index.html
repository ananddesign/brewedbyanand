02 — AI Products

Built, not prototyped

Products where the model is the mechanism, not the marketing. Each one ships with a working demo and public source.

Web App · AI product recognition

JUGL

A lightweight business OS for small sellers and pop-up businesses — inventory, live selling, expenses and customers, built around the moment of sale rather than the dashboard.

Why AI: Scan Item photographs a product and identifies it to log a sale — and surfaces low-confidence results for manual check instead of silently trusting the model.

Web App AI Vision QR Checkout

Next.js · Three.js · OpenAI

Imagio

An AI photography studio for jewellery brands. Upload one product shot, then change angle, lighting, lens, pose and background as easily as changing a setting — no new photoshoot.

Why AI: the model becomes the studio itself — generating a new shot from a real photograph, so the workflow is direct-and-iterate rather than prompt-and-hope.

Three.js OpenAI Supabase

Next.js · MapLibre · PostGIS

CityScope

A spatial exploration product for property decisions, where the map is the product. Every metro, park, project and news event becomes an entity you can point at and reason about.

The idea: a confidence lifecycle — Proposal → Approved → Tender → Construction → Operational — separates a genuine advantage from a speculative promise.

04 — About

About me

I’m Anand, a Product Designer with 9+ years of experience shaping products that drive meaningful growth through intentional design.

I specialise in 0-1 journeys, turning ambiguity into structured, scalable systems. My work spans fintech, e-commerce, SaaS, and luxury retail, with experience at PhonePe, Qween, Lollypop Design, and Fractal Ink.

I care deeply about craft, the design token that keeps a system honest, the flow that removes friction, the interaction that feels inevitable.

Currently at Qween, where I’m building design systems and crafting immersive product experiences for a luxury jewellery brand.

Always exploring, always building, there’s usually something new in the works.
